# Cashier · QR Payment Manual

## 1. Normal Payment Flow

1. Open the **Cashier** page
2. Select order on the left → click **💵 Collect**
3. Ask customer: **Which app are you using?**
4. Click the matching button:

   | Customer uses | Cashier clicks |
   |---|---|
   | ABA App | **ABA** |
   | ACLEDA App | **ACLEDA** |
   | Bakong App | either button |
   | Cash | Cash button |

5. Customer scans → confirms amount → pays
6. Verify receipt on your own phone → click **✅ Received**

---

## 2. Cash Payment

1. Customer pays cash → click **Cash**
2. Enter amount received
3. System computes change
4. Click **Confirm**

---

## 3. Refund

**Prerequisite**: order status must be **Settled**

1. Open order → click **💸 Refund**
2. Choose reason
3. Optional note
4. Confirm

---

## 4. FAQ

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| "QR invalid / no response" | Try the other button (ABA ↔ ACLEDA) |
| QR expired (10 min) | Close dialog → re-collect |
| Customer says paid, but no receipt | Check bank app → only confirm after verified |

---

## 5. Warnings

- ⚠️ Only confirm after customer's phone shows "Payment successful"
- ⚠️ Verify amount every transaction
- ⚠️ QR valid for 10 minutes
- ⚠️ Screenshot any anomaly, contact admin
